# Virasya - Website Questions & Prompts

The Virasya platform asks users a series of questions to help them preserve their cultural heritage and stories. Here are all the prompts and questions found across the platform, along with the expected answers or example data.

## 1. Preserve a Story (Preserve Page)

**Question:** What story do you carry? / What would you like to pass on?
**Answer (Expected):** The core memory or story the user wants to preserve. 
*Example:* "My grandmother used to say that every recipe begins with a story. When the monsoon came, she would make a pot of khichdi and call every neighbour home."

**Question:** Where is it from?
**Answer (Expected):** The geographical origin of the memory.
*Example:* "Puri, Odisha" or "Chettinad, Tamil Nadu".

**Question:** The Story (Write it how you remember it)
**Answer (Expected):** The detailed narrative text of the story or tradition.

---

## 2. Become a Host (Hosts Page)

**Question:** Who are you?
**Answer (Expected):** The name of the host.
*Example:* "Arjun Singh" or "Meera Rao".

**Question:** Where are you from?
**Answer (Expected):** The town or region they represent.
*Example:* "Varanasi, UP" or "Kutch, Gujarat".

**Question:** What is your tradition or role?
**Answer (Expected):** Their specific title or cultural function.
*Example:* "Instrument Maker", "Oral Historian", or "Master Weaver".

### Deep Dive Tradition Questions:

**Question:** Origin: Where does this tradition come from?
**Answer (Expected):** The historical or regional roots of the craft, song, or story.

**Question:** Journey: How did you personally learn or inherit it?
**Answer (Expected):** The personal lineage or apprenticeship experience of the host.

**Question:** Process: What materials, tools, or techniques are used?
**Answer (Expected):** Technical details, like the type of wood used for an instrument or the specific threads for a weave.

**Question:** Meaning: Why is it important to preserve this today?
**Answer (Expected):** The cultural significance and the threat of it being forgotten in the modern era.

---

## 3. General Search & Navigation

**Question:** Find what you remember. (Search Prompt)
**Answer (Expected):** Search keywords like places, people, or traditions.
*Example:* "Crafts", "Kerala", or "Lullabies".
